Privacy Policy — Is My Train Screwed?

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Is My Train Screwed? ("the Service") is an individually-operated, non-commercial NYC transit trip advisor, available via a web app and by text message (SMS/WhatsApp). This page explains what information the Service collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Your phone number, if you contact us by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • The content of messages you send us (e.g. your starting point, destination, and any trip details you share).
  • Trip details you ask us to monitor (e.g. your planned route and, if you give one, a deadline), so we can check for delays and let you know.
  • An anonymous identifier (not tied to your name) if you use the web app instead of text messaging.
  • Your device's location, only if you explicitly share it through the web app's location permission — we never ask for or receive device location over SMS/WhatsApp, since that's not technically possible over those channels.

How we use it

We use this information only to answer your trip questions, plan routes, check real-time transit conditions, and send you the disruption alerts you asked us to monitor. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Text messaging data

If you contact the Service by SMS or WhatsApp, your phone number and message content are used solely to operate the conversational trip-planning service you're using and to send you the specific trip updates you requested. We do not use your phone number for marketing, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties.

Service providers

Operating the Service requires passing your messages/trip data through a small number of third-party infrastructure providers strictly to deliver the service itself — for example, a cloud AI provider to generate trip-planning responses, a messaging provider to deliver SMS/WhatsApp messages, and MTA/NYC public transit data feeds to check real-time conditions. These providers process data only as needed to operate the Service on our behalf; none of them receive your information for their own marketing use.

Data retention

Conversation history and monitored-trip data are retained only as long as needed to provide the Service (e.g. while a trip you asked us to monitor is still active, or briefly afterward), and are not kept indefinitely.

Your choices

You can stop a monitored trip at any time by telling the Service (e.g. "stop monitoring" or "I made it"). Over SMS/WhatsApp, you can also reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from this number entirely, or HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ryushen@umich.edu.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
