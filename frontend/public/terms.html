Terms & Conditions — Is My Train Screwed?

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Is My Train Screwed? is an individually-operated, non-commercial NYC transit trip advisor, provided to you by an independent developer (not a company). It's available as a web app and by text message (SMS/WhatsApp).

What the Service does

The Service answers questions about NYC subway, bus, and ferry trips: it plans routes, reports real-time conditions, cites empirical transfer-reliability data where enough real observations exist, and can monitor a trip you're taking and text or message you if something changes.

Text messaging program

If you text the Service, you are opting in by sending us the first message — we never message you first unless you've asked the Service to monitor an active trip for disruptions.

  • Message frequency: varies based on your conversation and whether you're actively monitoring a trip; typically a few messages per trip.
  • Message and data rates may apply, per your mobile carrier plan.
  • Reply HELP at any time for help, or STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from this number.
  • Supported carriers may vary; the Service is not liable for undelivered messages due to carrier issues.

No guarantee of accuracy

The Service relies on public MTA real-time and historical data. Real-time conditions, delay predictions, and reliability percentages are based on actual observed data where available, but transit conditions can change unexpectedly. The Service is provided "as is," and we don't guarantee you'll make a specific connection or arrive by a specific time — always use your own judgment, especially for time-sensitive trips.

Acceptable use

Don't use the Service to send abusive, unlawful, or automated/bulk messages. We may block numbers that misuse the Service.

No cost to use

The Service itself is free to use (beyond your own mobile carrier's standard message/data rates, if you use it via SMS/WhatsApp).
